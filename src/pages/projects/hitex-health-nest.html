The brief

The clinic in Shilpa Hills covers pulmonology, diabetes care, acupuncture, chiropractic, yoga and lifestyle medicine under Dr. Bhaskar Reddy. It was new to being online. It needed a site that felt calm and trustworthy, and a way for patients to book without friction.

What I built

  • A three-step booking wizard that turns into a ready-to-send WhatsApp message, with a consent checkbox before sending
  • An offline AI receptionist that answers patient questions with no external API, checking for emergencies first
  • Service pages, the doctor's profile, a clinic gallery, FAQs, fees and directions
  • Light and dark themes

Beyond the website

Once the site was up, the more interesting questions were about everything around it. How does a new clinic get noticed? How should it present itself? How do the website, social media and WhatsApp hand a patient from one to the next?

  • Helped with the clinic's initial digital setup and how it presents itself online
  • Helped set up its Instagram presence and plan its first posts and content
  • Explored using ChatGPT and other AI tools to draft captions and test content ideas
  • Suggested marketing ideas and helped connect the tools where they were useful

I wasn't the clinic's marketing agency, and the decisions were the clinic's. But being involved beyond the code taught me to look at the whole problem a business has, not just the part I was asked to build.

Design

The look is blue liquid glass, with colours pulled from the clinic's logo. The signature detail is a slow breathing glow, four seconds in and six out, a nod to the clinic's lung care. I also made a logo intro animation and a paper-printing animation that plays when an appointment is booked.

What I learned

A clinic doesn't only need a website. It needs patients to find it, trust it and reach it. Seeing that pulled me into social media, content and messaging, and it's where I started thinking about technology as a set of connected systems rather than single projects.
