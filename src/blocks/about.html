Hi, I'm Hariswara.

I'm in Class 12 in Hyderabad, and I learn best when there is something real on the other end: a problem someone actually has, and something I can build to find out whether my idea holds up.

Technology became interesting to me when I realised I could take an idea and turn it into something people could use. Many of the apps I used every day felt like black boxes. Building my own showed me they are really a set of parts working together: an interface, a database, logins, APIs, messages going out on WhatsApp. Working out how those pieces connect is still my favourite part.

I rarely start a project knowing everything it needs. I start with the problem, find out what I have to learn, try things, break them and fix them. That is how I picked up Python, web apps, databases, AI APIs and automation, and it is why I want to study AI and machine learning properly from 2027.

Outside of building, I spent Classes 5 to 10 at a residential school where I played nearly every sport on offer. I've done 50 drives with the Robin Hood Army, and I volunteer at community events around the city.
