Edu Expos at Bhashyam Blooms

Every year our classroom turned into an exhibition. We picked a theme, built it with our teachers and presented it live to parents. Looking back, it was an early version of something I still enjoy: building a thing to explain an idea.

  1. Class 5
    Police Station

    A working station with a jail and a prisoner. I played the Sub-Inspector and gave the speech.

  2. Class 7
    Eco-friendly Home

    A model house that stays cool without air conditioning.

  3. Class 8
    Hyderabad Metro

    Our biggest build, with trains, the elevated line and the streets around it. I was on the core team.

  4. Class 9
    Cryptocurrency

    What it is, how Bitcoin started and what to think about before investing.
